spool · privacy

The Meta app “Spool” is used only by spool (Milan, Italy) to publish its own posts on its own Instagram account, @spool.mi. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it handles

Only the access token and the account ID of @spool.mi, which Meta issues when the account is connected. They are kept on spool’s own computers and are used only to publish spool’s posts. The app does not collect data about other people, does not read messages, and does not share anything with third parties or advertisers.

Data deletion

To remove the connection, remove the app under Meta Business Suite → Settings → Integrations → Connected apps, or write to curb-sledges.0x@icloud.com. The token and account ID are deleted within 30 days.

In italiano: l’app Meta «Spool» serve solo a spool (Milano) per pubblicare i propri post sul proprio account Instagram @spool.mi.
